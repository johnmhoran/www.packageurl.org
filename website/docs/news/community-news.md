# Package-URL Community News

Stay up to date with important news about the tools that implement PURL or
VERS and the organizations that use PURL or VERS (or both).

## IANA Registration for 'pkg' URI scheme
**Date**: July 27, 2026

**Details**: IANA has registered 'pkg' as a provisional URI scheme. See
https://www.iana.org/assignments/uri-schemes/prov/pkg. We will follow up with
IANA to register 'pkg' as a permanent URL scheme.
